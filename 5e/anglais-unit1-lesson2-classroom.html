A/ Vocabulary

Les objets de la classe et les couleurs. Comme dans la Lesson 1, apprends le mot et sa définition en anglais.

1. Classroom objects

WordDefinition
A desk It's the table where a student sits and writes.
A chair It's the seat where a student sits.
A board It's the big black or white surface where the teacher writes.
A piece of chalk It's the white stick used to write on the blackboard.
A duster It's the object used to clean the board.
A school bag It's the bag where a student puts his books and pens.
A ruler It's the object used to draw straight lines and measure.
A pen It's the object used to write with ink.
A pencil It's the object used to write or draw, and you can erase it.
A window It's the opening in the wall that lets the light in.

B/ Language function

★ Describing the classroom: the verb « to be »

Pour dire ce qu'il y a dans la classe et de quelle couleur c'est, on utilise le verbe to be (être) : is au singulier, are au pluriel.

1. Affirmative form

📘 La règle

Sujet + is / are + …

  • is avec it, he, she, ou un seul objet ;
  • are avec they, we, you, ou plusieurs objets.
SingularPlural
The board is green.The boards are green.
It is a red pen.They are red pens.
There is a desk in the classroom.There are twenty desks in the classroom.

2. Negative form

📘 La règle

On ajoute not après le verbe : is not / are not. À l'oral, on contracte : isn't / aren't.

AffirmativeNegative
The chalk is white.The chalk is not black. = The chalk isn't black.
The chairs are black.The chairs are not blue. = The chairs aren't blue.
There is a window.There is not a door. = There isn't a door.

3. Dialogue

A: What is this?
B: It is a ruler.

A: Is it red?
B: No, it isn't red. It is yellow.

A: Are the desks brown?
B: Yes, they are brown.

⚠️ L'erreur classique

En anglais, la couleur se place avant le nom : a red pen, et pas a pen red. Mais avec « to be », elle vient après : the pen is red.

🐴 Pour t'entraîner

Regarde autour de toi et fais trois phrases : une affirmative (The bag is blue.), une négative (The bag isn't green.) et une avec there are (There are two windows.).

C/ Practice

Cherche d'abord seule, à voix haute si tu peux. Ensuite, ouvre la correction.

Exercise 1 — What is it?

Trouve l'objet de la classe.

  1. It's the object used to clean the board. → …
  2. It's the table where a student sits and writes. → …
  3. It's the bag where a student puts his books and pens. → …
  4. It's the object used to write or draw, and you can erase it. → …
Voir la correction
  1. A duster
  2. A desk
  3. A school bag
  4. A pencil

Exercise 2 — Is or are?

Complète avec is ou are.

  1. The board … green.
  2. The chairs … black.
  3. There … a window in the classroom.
  4. There … thirty students.
  5. My pens … blue and red.
Voir la correction
  1. is
  2. are
  3. is
  4. are
  5. are

Un seul objet → is. Plusieurs → are.

Exercise 3 — Make it negative

Mets la phrase à la forme négative (forme courte).

  1. The ruler is red.
  2. The desks are white.
  3. There is a computer in the classroom.
  4. It is a pencil.
Voir la correction
  1. The ruler isn't red.
  2. The desks aren't white.
  3. There isn't a computer in the classroom.
  4. It isn't a pencil.

Exercise 4 — Colour + noun

Remets les mots dans l'ordre.

  1. pen / a / red
  2. bag / blue / my / is / school
  3. are / chairs / the / brown
Voir la correction
  1. a red pen
  2. My school bag is blue.
  3. The chairs are brown.

Exercise 5 — Describe your classroom

Lis la description, puis écris la tienne avec au moins une phrase négative.

This is my classroom. It is big. There is a green board and there are forty desks. The desks are brown. There are three windows. There isn't a computer. My school bag is blue and my ruler is yellow. I like my classroom!

Un modèle pour commencer
  • This is my classroom. It is …
  • There is a … / There are … desks.
  • The board is … . The chairs are … .
  • There isn't a … . The walls aren't … .
