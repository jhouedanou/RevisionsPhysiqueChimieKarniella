B/ Language function

★ Expressing obligation using must and have to

Pour dire qu'une chose est obligatoire (il faut la faire), utilise must ou have to.

1. With must

📘 La structure

Subject + must + verb

Must ne change jamais : pas de -s, même avec he, she, it.

SubjectExample
I / You / We / They + must + verbYou must wash your hands before cooking.
He / She / It + must + verbShe must cut the onions.

2. With have to or has to

📘 La structure

Subject + have to / has to + verb

have to : avec I, you, we, they. has to : avec he, she, it.

SubjectExample
I / You / We / They + have to + verbWe have to buy the ingredients.
He / She / It + has to + verbMy mother has to boil the water.

⚠️ Les deux fautes

  • Mettre to après must : You must to wash → You must wash.
  • Oublier has : She have to cook → She has to cook.

🐴 Deux façons de dire « devoir »

Must et have to veulent dire « devoir ». Après must, le verbe vient tout de suite. Have to, lui, garde toujours son to.

C/ Practice

Cherche d'abord seule, à voix haute si tu peux. Ensuite, ouvre la correction.

Exercise 1 — Have to or has to?

Complète avec have to ou has to.

  1. I … cut the onions.
  2. My sister … wash the leaves.
  3. We … buy salt at the market.
  4. Aya … stir the soup.
  5. They … help their mother.
Voir la correction
  1. have to
  2. has to
  3. have to
  4. has to
  5. have to

Exercise 2 — Must

Remets les mots dans l'ordre pour faire une phrase.

  1. must / You / your hands / wash
  2. the water / boil / She / must
  3. taste / We / the soup / must
Voir la correction
  1. You must wash your hands.
  2. She must boil the water.
  3. We must taste the soup.

Exercise 3 — Find the mistake

Chaque phrase a une faute. Écris la phrase correcte.

  1. She must to add salt.
  2. My brother have to wash the pot.
  3. He musts stir the soup.
Voir la correction
  1. She must add salt.
  2. My brother has to wash the pot.
  3. He must stir the soup.

Exercise 4 — Grandma's tchonron soup

Lis le dialogue, puis réponds aux questions en anglais.

Karniella: Grandma, I want to cook the tchonron soup. Help me, please!

Grandma: First, you must wash your hands.

Karniella: And then?

Grandma: You have to wash the bean leaves and cut the onions.

Karniella: Who has to boil the water?

Grandma: Your brother has to boil the water. Then we add the leaves and the groundnut powder.

Karniella: And the salt?

Grandma: You must add a little salt and stir the soup.

  1. What must Karniella do first?
  2. Who has to boil the water?
  3. What must Karniella add at the end?
Voir la correction
  1. She must wash her hands.
  2. Her brother has to boil the water.
  3. She must add a little salt.
À toi : réponds pour toi
  • What do you have to do at home? → I have to …
  • What must you do before cooking? → I must …
