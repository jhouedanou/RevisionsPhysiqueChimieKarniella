B/ Language function

★ Expressing an action in progress with: the present continuous

Le présent continu sert à dire ce qu'on est en train de faire, en ce moment même.

1. Affirmative form

📘 La structure

Subject + BE (am / is / are) + verb-ING + object

SubjectBEExample
IamI am writing on the board.
YouareYou are speaking English.
He / She / ItisShe is lining up.
We / You / TheyareThey are working hard.

Dans ton cahier :

  • I am writing on the board. (I = sujet)
  • You are speaking English. (You = sujet)

2. Negative form

📘 La structure

Subject + BE + NOT + verb-ING + object

  • I am not writing on the board. = I'm not writing…
  • You are not speaking French. = You aren't speaking French.
  • She is not lining up. = She isn't lining up.

⚠️ Les deux fautes classiques

  • Oublier BE : I writing → I am writing.
  • Oublier -ING : You are speak → You are speaking.

Rappel de la Lesson 1 : le e final tombe (write → writing) et certaines consonnes doublent (swim → swimming).

✏️ À vérifier dans ton cahier

Tes photos s'arrêtent à la forme affirmative (1). Dans le vocal, tu citais aussi « 2. Negative form » : je l'ai rédigée d'après la règle habituelle. Envoie la page suivante et je la remplace par ce qu'a écrit ton professeur.

C/ Practice

Cherche d'abord seule, à voix haute si tu peux. Ensuite, ouvre la correction.

Exercise 1 — Match

Donne l'explication en anglais.

  1. To stay down means… …
  2. To go up means… …
  3. To line up means… …
  4. To work hard means… …
  5. To be top of means… …
Voir la correction
  1. To repeat the class.
  2. To go to the next class.
  3. To queue up.
  4. To work a lot.
  5. To be the best of.

Exercise 2 — Opposites

Donne le contraire.

  1. to be late ≠ …
  2. to be top of ≠ …
  3. to go up ≠ …
Voir la correction
  1. to be on time
  2. to be bottom of
  3. to stay down

Exercise 3 — Am, is or are?

Complète avec am, is ou are.

  1. I … writing on the board.
  2. The students … lining up.
  3. Karniella … working hard.
  4. We … speaking English.
  5. The teacher … reading the reports.
Voir la correction
  1. am
  2. are
  3. is
  4. are
  5. is

Exercise 4 — Put the verb in the present continuous

  1. She (write) … a letter.
  2. They (play) … in the playground.
  3. I (read) … my school report.
  4. You (sit) … next to the window.
Voir la correction
  1. She is writing a letter.
  2. They are playing in the playground.
  3. I am reading my school report.
  4. You are sitting next to the window. (sit → sitting : le t double)

Exercise 5 — Correct the mistake

  1. I writing on the board.
  2. You are speak English.
  3. They is lining up.
Voir la correction
  1. I am writing on the board.
  2. You are speaking English.
  3. They are lining up.

Exercise 6 — Report day

Lis à voix haute, puis raconte ta propre journée des bulletins.

Teacher: Line up, please! I am giving the school reports.

Teacher: Karniella, you are top of the class. Well done!

Karniella: Thank you, sir! I am working hard.

Teacher: You are going up to 4e. Nobody is staying down this year!

Mots utiles
  • sir : monsieur (au professeur) ; madam : madame
  • nobody : personne
  • this year : cette année
