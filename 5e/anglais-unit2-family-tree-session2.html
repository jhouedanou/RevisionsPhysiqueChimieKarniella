B/ Language function

★ Expressing immediate future with « be going to »

Pour dire ce que tu vas faire bientôt, utilise be going to. C'est le futur proche.

1. Affirmative form

📘 La structure

Subject + BE (am / is / are) + going to + verb

Subject + BEExample
I + amI am going to visit my grandparents tomorrow.
You + areYou are going to clean your bedroom this evening.
He / She / It + isShe is going to cook rice tonight.
We / You / They + areWe are going to watch TV in the living room.

À l'oral, on raccourcit : I'm going to, she's going to, we're going to.

2. Negative form

📘 La structure

Subject + BE + NOT + going to + verb

  • I am not going to watch TV tonight.
  • My brother isn't going to come tomorrow.

3. Interrogative form

📘 La structure

BE + subject + going to + verb ?

On met BE devant le sujet, comme au présent continu.

A: Are you going to visit your uncle next week?

B: Yes, I am.

A: What are you going to do this weekend?

B: I'm going to play with my cousins.

4. Les mots du futur

WordEn français
tomorrowdemain
tonight / this eveningce soir
this weekendce week-end
next weekla semaine prochaine
next Sundaydimanche prochain
soonbientôt

⚠️ Les deux fautes

  • Oublier BE : I going to visit → I am going to visit.
  • Changer le verbe : She is going to cooks → She is going to cook.

🐴 Comme en français

Going to marche comme « aller + verbe ». Je vais visiter → I am going to visit.

C/ Practice

Cherche d'abord seule, à voix haute si tu peux. Ensuite, ouvre la correction.

Exercise 1 — What is it?

Trouve le mot qui correspond.

  1. The son of your brother or of your sister. → …
  2. The daughter of your son or of your daughter. → …
  3. It's the room where we cook food. → …
  4. It's the room where we sleep. → …
  5. It's the room where we take a shower. → …
Voir la correction
  1. A nephew
  2. A granddaughter
  3. A kitchen
  4. A bedroom
  5. A bathroom

Exercise 2 — Aya's family tree

Regarde l'arbre de l'onglet Vocabulary et complète.

  1. Ama is Amenan's ….
  2. Koffi is Adjoua's ….
  3. Aya is Konan's ….
  4. Aya, Koffi and Ama are Kouassi's ….
  5. Ama has no brother and no sister. She is an ….
Voir la correction
  1. granddaughter
  2. nephew
  3. niece
  4. grandchildren
  5. only child

Exercise 3 — Be going to

Complète avec be going to et le verbe entre parenthèses.

  1. I … (visit) my grandparents next Sunday.
  2. My mother … (cook) rice this evening.
  3. We … (watch) a film in the living room tonight.
  4. Koffi and Aya … (play) football tomorrow.
  5. You … (clean) the kitchen after lunch.
Voir la correction
  1. am going to visit
  2. is going to cook
  3. are going to watch
  4. are going to play
  5. are going to clean

Exercise 4 — Negative and question

Écris chaque phrase à la forme négative, puis à la forme interrogative.

  1. She is going to sleep.
  2. They are going to visit their uncle.
  3. You are going to cook tonight.
Voir la correction
  1. She isn't going to sleep. — Is she going to sleep?
  2. They aren't going to visit their uncle. — Are they going to visit their uncle?
  3. You aren't going to cook tonight. — Are you going to cook tonight?

Exercise 5 — Role play

Karniella et Aya parlent du week-end. Joue la scène.

Karniella: What are you going to do this weekend, Aya?

Aya: I'm going to visit my grandparents in the village.

Karniella: Are your parents going to come with you?

Aya: Yes, they are. My aunt is going to cook for the family.

Karniella: Is your cousin Ama going to be there?

Aya: Of course! We're going to play together.

À toi : réponds pour toi
  • What are you going to do this weekend? → I'm going to …
  • Are you going to visit your family? → Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
